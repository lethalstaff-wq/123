## payments stack

### Вывод

Hard constraint that decides everything: Stripe is NOT available in any CIS country (50 supported countries as of 2026-09, zero CIS; India/Indonesia preview only), and Stripe's business origin country cannot be changed after live activation. So a CIS operator has exactly three legal paths: (A) a foreign legal entity (the market leader EXM Tweaks does exactly this — EXM TWEAKS s.r.o., Slovakia, "Payments are processed by Stripe", per its Refund Policy dated 2026-08-19), (B) a merchant-of-record that pays out to a CIS bank, or (C) crypto. On (B), Lemon Squeezy (Stripe-owned) is the only MoR that publishes bank payouts to CIS: Armenia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Moldova, Uzbekistan are on its bank-payout list (Georgia, Ukraine, Russia, Belarus, Kyrgyzstan are NOT; Russia is also blocked as a BUYER country), fee 5% + $0.50 +1.5% intl +0.5% subscriptions ≈ 7% + $0.50 all-in, and its prohibited list does not ban PC optimizers. Paddle must be ruled out: its AUP (updated 2026-04-13) explicitly lists "Technical support services, including any software or service, marketed to repair, maintain, or improve the performance or security of an Electronic Device" and "System Health Products... Device Cleaners" as prohibited/restricted — that is a verbatim description of this product. Gumroad bans "hacking and cracking materials" and charges 10% + $0.50. PayPal is usable as a secondary method and, per its Rest-of-World fee schedule (updated 2026-05-28), does publish commercial RECEIVING rates for KZ/GE/AM/AZ/MD/KG/TJ/TM (4.40–5.39% + fixed) — but PayPal nowhere documents per-market receive-vs-send-only capability, so treat CIS receiving as unverified; PayPal's real cost is the dispute fee ($15 standard / $30 high-volume) plus a contractual right to raise your rate on 30 days notice if complaints run high. Crypto is the honest answer to the "key question": global crypto ownership is ~6.8% (Triple-A, 2024 data, 560M owners) and skews male 25-34; a 13-24 low-ticket gaming audience will convert in the low single digits on crypto, so crypto is a margin-saving supplement (NOWPayments 1%, BitPay 1%+25c, no chargebacks), never the primary rail. The single best-kept secret in the stack: Stripe's own stablecoin payments support recurring billing YES and dispute support NO — chargeback-immune subscriptions, global customers, USD, settled to your Stripe balance in local currency. Local APMs are largely a trap for a subscription business: on Stripe, Pix/UPI/PayNow/PromptPay are not supported in Checkout subscription mode (Subscriptions/Invoices only via send_invoice), and GCash, GoPay, mada, STC Pay, M-Pesa, Papara, Kaspi and SBP do not exist on Stripe at all. Recommended: PRIMARY = non-CIS entity (Stripe Atlas Delaware LLC, $500 + $100/yr, or an EU s.r.o./Ltd) on Stripe direct; BACKUP = Lemon Squeezy with CIS bank payout; THIRD = crypto (NOWPayments/Stripe stablecoins) for the crypto-native slice and as an outage fallback. Chargeback discipline is existential, not cosmetic: Visa VAMP flags you as non-compliant at a 0.5% dispute ratio with only 5 disputes, and Mastercard ECM fines start at 100 disputes + 1.5% and escalate to $25,000-$50,000 by months 4-5.

### Правила и константы для системы

- CONSTANT STRIPE_SUPPORTED_COUNTRIES = the 50-country list from stripe.com/global (AU AT BE BR BG CA HR CY CZ DK EE FI FR DE GI GR HK HU IE IT JP LV LI LT LU MY MT MX NL NZ NO PL PT RO SG SK SI ES SE CH TH AE GB US). Used to validate the OPERATOR's entity country, never the customer's.
- CONSTANT LS_BANK_PAYOUT_CIS = [Armenia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Moldova, Uzbekistan]. If operator_country is in this set, the Lemon Squeezy backup rail is viable with a local bank; otherwise the backup must use PayPal payout or a foreign entity.
- CONSTANT BLOCKED_BUYER_COUNTRIES = [CF, CU, KP, CD, ER, GW, IR, IQ, LB, LY, ML, RU, SO, SS, SD, SY, YE]. Enforce at three layers: (1) checkout geo-gate, (2) licence activation, (3) ad-targeting exclusion list for the auto-upload network. Do not spend impressions on traffic that cannot pay.
- RULE: operator entity country is IMMUTABLE after first live Stripe charge. The spec must require operator_entity_country as a required config field set before any live key is installed, and refuse to run in live mode if it is unset.
- CONSTANT DISPUTE_RATIO_LIMITS = {visa_noncompliant_ratio: 0.005, visa_noncompliant_count: 5, visa_excessive_ratio: 0.015, visa_excessive_ratio_cemea: 0.022, mc_ecm_count: 100, mc_ecm_ratio: 0.015, mc_hecm_count: 300, mc_hecm_ratio: 0.03}. The ops dashboard must compute rolling monthly dispute_count and dispute_count/captured_payments_prev_month and alert at 60% of the 0.5%/5-count trigger.
- RULE: 3DS must be enabled such that 3DS payments stay above 10% of Mastercard volume — this alone removes one of the four mandatory EFM conditions. Make it a monitored metric, not a one-time setting.
- CONSTANT COST_MODEL per rail (for price/margin calc): stripe_direct_intl = 0.0315*amount + 0.20 GBP + 0.02*amount FX; lemon_squeezy_card_sub_intl = 0.07*amount + 0.50 USD + 0.01 payout; gumroad = 0.10*amount + 0.50; paypal_intl_uk = 0.029*amount + 0.20 GBP; paypal_row_kz = 0.044*amount + 0.30 USD; crypto_nowpayments = 0.01*amount; bitpay = 0.01*amount + 0.25.
- CONSTANT DISPUTE_COST per rail: stripe = 20 GBP received + 20 GBP countered; paypal = 15 USD standard / 30 USD high-volume; paddle = up to 20 GBP/USD/EUR incl. pre-chargeback alerts. Feed into a break-even guard: refund_threshold_amount below which auto-refunding is always cheaper than disputing.
- RULE: APM/local-method SKUs must be PREPAID DURATION CODES (30/90/365 day), never subscriptions, because Stripe does not support Pix/UPI/PayNow/PromptPay/Boleto/OXXO/Konbini/Multibanco in Checkout subscription mode (Subscriptions/Invoices only via send_invoice).
- RULE: two product families in the catalog — family A (cards, wallets, Link, stablecoins) = recurring subscription; family B (local APMs, vouchers, non-Stripe crypto) = one-time prepaid code. Entitlement duration, not billing cycle, is the canonical field.
- RULE: crypto checkout option visibility is geo-weighted. Surface prominently for IN, PK, VN, BR, NG, ID, UA, PH, TR, BD, ET, VE, TH; de-emphasize for US, GB, DE, FR, CA, AU, NL, SE. Expected crypto share: model 2-6% of checkouts (low confidence), not 20%+.
- RULE: single internal event 'entitlement.granted {order_id, user_id, rail, sku, duration_days, hwid_slots}' drives ALL auto-delivery (licence key issue, HWID bind, Discord role grant). Per-rail webhook adapters normalize into it. Never wire Discord role grants directly to a provider webhook payload shape.
- RULE: for every order persist a dispute-evidence bundle: checkout IP + country, timestamp, user agent, the exact landing-page variant id, the timestamped EU/UK withdrawal-waiver consent, licence key issuance time, HWID bind event, Discord role grant event, first software launch telemetry, and every support message. This bundle is both the Stripe dispute response and the PayPal Seller Protection claim for intangible goods.
- RULE: statement descriptor must be set explicitly and shown to the buyer on the thank-you page AND in the delivery email ('Your bank statement will show: <DESCRIPTOR>'). Unrecognized descriptors are a primary friendly-fraud trigger for a 13-24 audience paying on a parent's card.
- RULE: self-serve instant refund available in-app and via Discord bot for orders under a configurable amount (suggest <= 2x monthly price) with no support queue and no proof required. Log it as refund_reason. Target: refund path strictly faster and lower-friction than opening a bank/PayPal dispute.
- RULE: self-serve instant cancellation of recurring billing, exposed in-app, in the Discord bot, and linked in every renewal email sent 3-7 days BEFORE each charge. 'I forgot I was subscribed' is the dominant friendly-fraud reason on cheap subs — consider following the category leader and selling one-time licences instead of subscriptions.
- CONSTANT REFUND_POLICY_CLAUSES (ship near-verbatim, derived from the category leader's published policy): (1) named seller legal entity + country + 'payments processed by <PSP>'; (2) refund grounds incl. won't run on your system, defect prevents use, no worthwhile improvement on your machine, MINOR PURCHASED WITHOUT PARENTAL CONSENT, duplicate/accidental purchase; (3) no proof, benchmarks or screenshots required; (4) no fixed deadline, reviewed individually; (5) decline grounds: licence shared/resold/unauthorized activation, repeated-refund pattern across accounts/payment-methods/HWIDs, stated reason contradicted by our own activation and usage records; (6) EU/UK 14-day withdrawal + express immediate-access waiver + 'nothing limits mandatory consumer law'; (7) chargeback clause: contact us before your bank, access suspended for the dispute duration, permanent ban + no further refund + possible collections referral for fraudulent or post-refund chargebacks, repeated cross-account refund requests treated as abuse; (8) affiliate commission auto-voided and reversed on refund or chargeback; (9) refunds to ORIGINAL payment method only, 5-10 business days, licence deactivated on refund; (10) 'the policy version that applies is the one published on the date you bought'.
- CONSTANT CHECKOUT_REQUIRED_CONSENTS = [withdrawal_waiver_checkbox (unticked by default, EU/UK), age_confirmation (18+ or parental consent), tos_accept, refund_policy_accept]. Persist each with timestamp + policy version hash per order.
- RULE: marketing-copy compliance linter over all landing pages, ad creatives and video descriptions. BLOCK: guaranteed FPS figures, absolute '+N FPS' claims, fabricated testimonials or review counts, countdown/scarcity upsell timers, 'remote tech support' framing. Rationale: Stripe prohibits 'outrageous claims', 'deceptive testimonials', 'high-pressure upselling', 'no-value-added services' and 'suspicious remote technical support'. Archive every published variant for dispute evidence.
- RULE: hard product-scope separation. Game accounts, in-game currency and in-game items must NEVER share a legal entity, PSP account, domain or Discord server with the tweaks subscription. Stripe restricts in-game item sales to the game operator and prohibits IP infringement; PayPal requires pre-approval for virtual in-game currency; PayPal links accounts across name/address/phone/email/card/bank/device/geolocation. One category violation would terminate the entire revenue stream.
- RULE: one payment identity per legal entity. The 20-channels-per-geo social strategy must NOT be mirrored on the payments side. Payment-account sprawl triggers PayPal linked-account termination and Stripe's undisclosed-merchant prohibition.
- EXCLUDE Paddle from the rail candidate list entirely: its AUP (2026-04-13) names 'any software or service, marketed to repair, maintain, or improve the performance or security of an Electronic Device' and 'System Health Products... Device Cleaners' as prohibited/restricted. Do not build an integration.
- GAP / DO NOT FABRICATE: no fee, prohibited-products or payout-country data could be retrieved for Sellix, Shoppy, Whop or Payhip (sellix.io blocked by egress policy; whop.com and its help/dev subdomains return a JS SPA shell on every route; payhip.com returns 403; shoppy.gg Wayback blocked). Leave these as null in the spec and flag for manual human verification — Whop especially, as it is the default paid-Discord platform in this niche.
- GAP: no primary-source pricing or CIS-availability data for the aggregators that actually carry GCash, GoPay, mada, STC Pay, M-Pesa, Papara, Kaspi or SBP (dLocal 404, Xsolla pricing JS-rendered, FastSpring sales-gated). Treat all non-Stripe local-APM coverage as unpriced until verified.
- GAP: PayPal publishes commercial RECEIVING rates for KZ/GE/AM/AZ/MD/KG/TJ/TM but nowhere documents per-market receive-vs-send-only capability, and qualifies bank withdrawal with 'where available'. Spec must require an empirical $1 receive + withdraw test before treating a CIS PayPal business account as a live payout path.
- GAP: Stripe Atlas publishes no country-eligibility or excluded-nationality list; docs.stripe.com/atlas/faq is 404. Whether RU/BY-resident founders can pass Atlas + Stripe verification is undocumented. Require an onboarding attempt before architecting around it.
- GAP: no direct data found on what share of a 13-24 gaming audience can actually pay in crypto. Global ownership is 6.8% skewed to men 25-34, and under-18s generally cannot pass exchange KYC. Model 2-6% with LOW CONFIDENCE; instrument the real number from day one via per-rail checkout-initiated vs completed events.

### Риски

- EXISTENTIAL: Stripe does not operate in any CIS country and the business origin country locks permanently at first live charge. Without a foreign entity the primary rail simply does not exist, and there is no later migration that preserves subscriptions.
- HIGH: Paddle explicitly prohibits/restricts 'any software or service marketed to repair, maintain, or improve the performance or security of an Electronic Device' and 'System Health Products' (AUP 2026-04-13). Onboarding there would likely be rejected or terminated after revenue had already accumulated, with fees retained at Paddle's discretion.
- HIGH: Stripe can terminate for marketing style rather than product category. 'Outrageous claims', 'deceptive testimonials', 'high-pressure upselling', 'no-value-added services' and 'suspicious remote technical support' are all named prohibited conduct. Typical FPS-boost landing pages ('+250 FPS guaranteed', fake review counters, countdown timers) sit directly inside those clauses.
- HIGH: chargeback math is brutal at this price point. Visa VAMP flags non-compliance at a 0.5% ratio with just 5 disputes/month; a store doing 200 sales/month can absorb roughly one dispute. Mastercard ECM fines reach $25,000-$50,000 by months 4-5. A 13-24 audience paying on parents' cards is the highest-friendly-fraud demographic there is.
- HIGH: per-dispute cost exceeds transaction value by 1.5-4x. Stripe £20 + £20, PayPal $15 rising to $30 for high-volume dispute accounts, Paddle up to 20 GBP/USD/EUR including pre-chargeback ALERTS (charged even when no chargeback lands). On a $10 sub, each dispute erases 3-6 sales.
- MEDIUM-HIGH: PayPal can quietly raise your percentage fees on 30 days notice for disproportionate complaints, and can freeze funds. Its Restricted Activities also ban controlling an account linked to a violating one, with linkage inferred from name, address, phone, email, card, bank, device and geolocation.
- MEDIUM-HIGH: merchant-of-record means losing control of refunds and pricing. Paddle reserves the right to set the retail price and to unilaterally refund buyers; Lemon Squeezy can place a store 'in review or suspended without notice'. A carefully drafted refund policy is only partially enforceable under an MoR.
- MEDIUM: single-rail dependency. Any one processor decision (rejection, review, freeze, category reclassification) stops 100% of revenue instantly, while the traffic machine keeps burning budget. Without a live, tested secondary rail the whole funnel is a single point of failure.
- MEDIUM: crypto cannot carry this audience. Global ownership is 6.8%, skewed to men 25-34, and under-18 buyers generally cannot pass exchange KYC. Building the business on a crypto-first checkout would sharply cut conversion. Treat the 'crypto solves the CIS problem' idea as a myth at this audience and price point.
- MEDIUM: local payment methods do not support recurring billing on Stripe (Pix, UPI, PayNow, PromptPay, Boleto, OXXO, Konbini, Multibanco all excluded from Checkout subscription mode), and GCash, GoPay, mada, STC Pay, M-Pesa, Papara, Kaspi and SBP do not exist on Stripe at all. Any 'local methods everywhere' plan needs a second processor and a second product model.
- MEDIUM: EU/UK consumers have an unconditional 14-day withdrawal right on digital content unless an express immediate-access waiver is captured at checkout. Without that checkbox, every EU/UK sale is refundable on demand for 14 days no matter what the policy says — and the UK/EU is the stated positioning market.
- MEDIUM: geo-blocking obligations are real and asymmetric. Lemon Squeezy blocks 18 buyer countries including Russia; Stripe prohibits supplying IT-support and cloud services to any person located in Russia. A traffic strategy that reaches RU/BY audiences converts into compliance exposure, not revenue.
- MEDIUM: the game-accounts expansion is incompatible with card rails. Stripe restricts in-game item/currency sales to the game operator and prohibits IP infringement; PayPal requires pre-approval for virtual in-game currency and prohibits stolen digital goods. Beyond platform rules, selling accounts breaches Epic/Riot/Valve terms. Co-locating it with the tweaks business risks losing both.
- LOW-MEDIUM: Paddle's $100 monthly payout floor and mid-following-month payment cycle create a real cash-flow gap for an operator running a $50-200/mo infrastructure budget — early months could accrue no payout at all.
- LOW-MEDIUM: no verified data exists for the four platforms most native to this niche (Sellix, Shoppy, Whop, Payhip). Building the spec on assumed fees or assumed policy for any of them would inject fabricated constants into the auto-delivery system.
- LOW: micropayment pricing on PayPal applies account-wide once granted (5%/6% + fixed). If the catalog mixes a £4 monthly plan with a £60 annual plan, opting in makes the high-ticket transactions materially worse.
- LOW: US tax compliance for a foreign-owned Delaware LLC (Form 5472 + 1120, with substantial penalties for non-filing) is not covered by the $500 Atlas fee and is an easy thing for a solo non-US operator to miss entirely.

### Открытые вопросы

- Can a Kazakhstan / Georgia / Armenia / Uzbekistan / Azerbaijan PayPal BUSINESS account actually receive and withdraw commercial payments, or is it send-only in practice? PayPal publishes receiving rates for these markets (4.40-5.39% + fixed, updated 2026-05-28) but no per-market capability table, and qualifies withdrawals with 'where available'. Only a $1 live test settles it.
- Will Stripe Atlas + Stripe verification accept a founder resident in Kazakhstan, Georgia, Armenia, Uzbekistan or Moldova? And is it effectively closed to Russian and Belarusian residents? Atlas publishes no eligibility list and its FAQ page is 404.
- What are Whop's actual fees, prohibited-products policy and payout countries? It is the category-native platform for paid Discord communities with built-in role auto-delivery, and it is the single biggest unmeasured option in this stack. - Does Lemon Squeezy's underwriting actually accept a Windows performance-optimization utility? - What is the effective VAT/sales-tax burden if going Stripe-direct rather than MoR? - Does Discord's own ToS or monetization policy restrict selling paid roles for game-modification software, and could a Discord ban destroy the delivery mechanism independently of the payment rail? Not researched in this dimension.
- Which statement descriptor and brand name minimize 'I don't recognize this charge' disputes for a parent reviewing a teenager's spending? Untested — worth an A/B test on descriptor wording, since descriptor confusion is a leading friendly-fraud cause.

- PRICING_GRID_VARIANT_A (Subscription-first, для MRR; требует Stripe или Whop, т.к. Paddle не берёт <$10): free tier $0 (базовые твики, встроенный FPS-бенчмарк до/после); CORE monthly $9.99 T1, quarterly $24.99 (2.5× monthly), annual $44.99 (4.5× monthly, −62% против 12×); PRO monthly $14.99, annual $69.99, 2 устройства; LIFETIME PRO $119 — показывать ТОЛЬКО в трёх точках: (1) в cancel-flow, (2) на 3-м месяце активной подписки, (3) сезонная кампания. Paid trial $1 / 3 дня с обязательной картой. Money-back 7 дней без вопросов. Обоснование: annual-множитель из медиан RevenueCat 2026 (3.48–4.00×); paid trial вместо free — потому что твики остаются применёнными после отмены, а Stripe прямо рекомендует подтверждение billing terms до оплаты как антидиспутную меру
- PRICING_GRID_VARIANT_B (Lifetime-first, зеркало проверенной модели EXM; рекомендуется как стартовая): free tier $0; PLUS lifetime $29.99 (1 ПК) / $54.99 (2) / $74.99 (3) / $99.99 (4); PREMIUM lifetime $49.99 / $89.99 / $129.99 / $169.99 — цифры взяты 1:1 из работающего EXM (532 отзыва, 4.6/5); сверху опциональный recurring слой $4.99/мес «live-тюнинг + профили под новые патчи игр + приоритет в Discord», который и есть источник MRR. Обоснование: максимальная касса с холодного short-form клика без обязательства повторять продажу; Paddle при чеке $29.99+ стоит 6.7% и снимает всю налоговую отчётность с одного оператора
- PRICING_GRID_VARIANT_C (Gaming-native weekly, самый рискованный): weekly $4.99 T1 / monthly $12.99 / annual $59.99 / lifetime $99. Trial 2 дня за $1. Обоснование: Gaming выбирает weekly в 82% случаев (RevenueCat 2026). ПРОТИВОПОКАЗАНИЕ: churn первого продления weekly 42–65%, Y1 retention weekly 3.4%, фиксированная комиссия 20p–50¢ на чеке $4.99 = 4–10% только фикса, и частые мелкие списания на картах подростков — прямой путь к превышению VAMP count=5. Использовать только как A/B на одном гео-сегменте, не как основную сетку
- GEO_TIERS: T1=1.00, T2=0.80, T3=0.60, T4=0.40 от T1-цены. Классификатор по World Bank NY.GNP.PCAP.PP.CD ratio к USA: ≥0.65→T1; 0.45–0.65→T2; 0.25–0.45→T3; <0.25→T4. Готовое распределение: T1 = US CA GB AU NZ DE NL SE NO DK CH IE AT FI BE FR SG JP KR IL AE SA IT ES; T2 = PL CZ RO HU SK SI EE LV LT HR BG PT GR TR TW HK CL UY; T3 = BR MX AR CO PE KZ MY TH RS GE AM UZ; T4 = IN ID PH VN PK BD EG NG KE MA LK NP UA ZA. HARD FLOOR: не опускать ниже 0.40 от T1 (наблюдаемая рыночная норма IN/SEA 38–46% от NA по RevenueCat 2026). Таблицу хранить статическим JSON, обновлять раз в год из api.worldbank.org/v2/country/all/indicator/NY.GNP.PCAP.PP.CD?mrnev=1
- GEO_IMPLEMENTATION: гео-тиры НЕ реализуются через Stripe currency_options (там ключ — валюта, не страна). Нужны 4 отдельных набора Price-объектов + серверный выбор тира. Определять тир по стране платёжного метода/BIN на этапе подтверждения платежа, а не только по IP (иначе T4 уйдёт всем через VPN). RU и BY отсутствуют в списке поддерживаемых стран Adaptive Pricing

- DO_NOT_MODEL (нет данных, не закладывать в базовый план выручки): attach rate физических апселлов (коврики, ноутбуки, мини-ПК) — для сценарного моделирования low-confidence диапазон 1–4%/мес на аксессуар $25–45 и 0.1–0.5% на устройства; эффект скидок на churn (коэффициент не подставлять, диапазон удержания скидочной когорты 0.8–1.0× от полной); доля lifetime в структуре продаж — из данных о выборе плана (42% monthly / 34% yearly / 16% weekly) остаётся ~8% на lifetime и прочее, low confidence; доля выручки от lifetime при цене в 3–5× годовой — оценочно 20–35%, low confidence

### Риски

- ГЛАВНЫЙ РИСК МОДЕЛИ: порог Visa VAMP по количеству — 5 диспутов в месяц при VAMP ratio 0.5%. При объёме 150–600 транзакций/мес практический потолок = 4 диспута в месяц. Аудитория 13–24 платит картами родителей — это классический источник friendly fraud и chargeback'ов «я этого не покупал». Один плохой месяц с 6–8 диспутами ставит под угрозу эквайринг. Это ограничение жёстче любого маркетингового решения и должно быть в коде как блокирующая константа.
- Weekly-планы, которые статистически предпочитает геймерская аудитория (82%), одновременно и максимально дисперсны по диспутам (частые мелкие списания на детской карте), и дают худшее удержание (Y1 retention 3.4%), и максимально страдают от фиксированной части комиссии (20p–50¢ на чеке $4.99). Соблазн скопировать «82% weekly» может убить эквайринг.
- Monthly-only модель — беговая дорожка: для $10k net/мес нужно 368–589 новых подписок КАЖДЫЙ месяц только на удержание уровня при churn 25–40%. Если сеть каналов просядет (баны, изменение алгоритма, выгорание креативов), MRR начнёт падать на 25–40% в месяц без возможности быстро восстановиться. Lifetime-выручка от падения трафика не проседает так же резко (уже полученные деньги не отзываются).
- Гео-тиры без привязки к стране платёжного метода будут арбитражированы через VPN — целевая аудитория технически подкована и находится в Discord, где такие обходы распространяются мгновенно. Итог: значительная часть T1-трафика оплатит по T4 (0.40×), что обрушит blended-множитель с 0.79 до 0.45–0.55 и сломает всю юнит-экономику.
- Adaptive Pricing не поддерживает локальные методы оплаты для cross-border подписок (только card/Link/Apple Pay/Google Pay). В T3/T4 гео, где карта менее распространена, подписка будет конвертить существенно хуже ожидаемого — а именно оттуда придёт основная часть дешёвого short-form трафика.
- Paddle не принимает товары дешевле $10 без индивидуального согласования — если сетка стартует с $5–9.99, единственный MoR-вариант выпадает, и вся налоговая нагрузка (VAT/sales tax по десяткам юрисдикций) ложится на одного оператора из СНГ. Это не риск цены, а риск легальной неработоспособности схемы.
- Копирование lifetime-модели EXM означает отсутствие MRR. При lifetime-only выручка полностью равна потоку новых продаж: любой провал трафика = немедленный провал выручки, оценочная стоимость бизнеса ниже, кассовый поток нестабилен. При этом подписка в этой нише плохо оправдана ценностно: твики применяются один раз и продолжают работать после отмены — пользователь резонно спросит, за что платит второй месяц. Это ядровая проблема упаковки, а не вопрос цены.
- Отзывы на Trustpilot у EXM упоминают конфликты с античитами. Для игр из списка (Valorant/Vanguard, Fortnite/EAC, CS2/VAC, Rust/EAC) любой твик, затрагивающий ядро или память, может привести к бану аккаунта пользователя. Это порождает не просто возвраты, а требования компенсации стоимости аккаунта, публичные скандалы в Discord и репутационный отток — плюс прямой рост диспутов.
- LTV low-priced подписчика по данным рынка $7–15 (RLTV per payer monthly low-priced $6.67, Gaming Y1 revenue per payer $11.22). Любая платная закупка трафика при таком LTV почти наверняка убыточна. Модель жизнеспособна только на бесплатном органическом short-form трафике — и это делает её полностью зависимой от политики площадок по мульти-аккаунтингу.
- Расширение в продажу игровых аккаунтов (упомянуто в контексте) несовместимо с сеткой подписок на том же юрлице и том же платёжном аккаунте: это нарушает правила Epic/Riot/Valve, а по политикам платёжных провайдеров ведёт к повышенному риску блокировки. Смешивание в одном Stripe/Paddle-аккаунте ставит под угрозу легальную часть бизнеса — эти потоки нельзя объединять.
- Цифры Recurly по churn помечены на странице как annual, но при значении 3.60% это даёт нереалистичное 99%+ годовое удержание; исторически Recurly публикует эти бенчмарки как месячные. Если взять их как годовые, модель удержания будет завышена в разы. В расчётах я трактую их как месячные — но это неустранённая неоднозначность источника.

### Открытые вопросы

- ЧЕСТНОЕ ОГРАНИЧЕНИЕ РАБОТЫ: требование о 10–16 поисковых запросах выполнить не удалось — бюджет WebSearch в этой сессии был исчерпан (200/200) ещё до моего первого вызова, все 4 первых запроса вернули отказ. Вместо поиска я сделал ~35 прямых WebFetch/curl-запросов по известным первоисточникам (Stripe Docs, Paddle, Gumroad, RevenueCat, Recurly, ChartMogul, Steamworks, Trustpilot, World Bank API, docs.whop.com) плюс прямой парсинг exmtweaks.com. Следствие: карта конкурентов в нише неполна — я подтвердил цены только одного (EXM). Если нужен полноценный конкурентный срез цен, задачу надо перезапустить с поднятым CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_WEB_SEARCHES_PER_SESSION.
- Take rate Whop не подтверждён из первоисточника (whop.com/pricing отдаёт SPA, help.whop.com/en/articles/whop-fees и docs.whop.com/sell/fees — 404). Это несущая константа юнит-экономики при чеке $9.99. Нужно уточнить до выбора процессора.
- Цены прямых конкурентов кроме EXM не собраны. Домены, которые я проверил и которые оказались нерелевантны/недоступны: exitlag.com (403), noping.com (403), wtfast.com/en/plans (404), bitsum.com/order (404), haste.gg (теперь крипто-игра, не сетевой оптимизатор), outfox.com (редиректит на merkle.se). Нужен срез по 5–10 реальным конкурентам: их сетка, наличие подписки vs lifetime, гео-цены, наличие trial.
- Доля lifetime-покупок в общем объёме продаж там, где lifetime предлагается наряду с подпиской — прямых данных нет. RevenueCat даёт только распространённость оффера (18% gaming-paywall) и распределение выбора подписочных планов (42/34/16), откуда lifetime+прочее ≈ 8% юнитов. Нужна реальная цифра, чтобы правильно взвесить гибридную сетку.
- Эффект скидок на churn для потребительских подписок — все известные мне URL с данными ProfitWell/Paddle на эту тему сейчас 404/410. Нужен живой источник или собственный A/B.
- Refund rate для этой конкретной нише (PC-твики, аудитория 13–24) — нет данных. Я использую оценку 5–6%; реальная цифра критична, т.к. при нарушении античит-совместимости возвраты могут быть в разы выше. Отзывы на Trustpilot упоминают конфликты с античитами.
- Ценовая чувствительность и предпочтения Gen Z по monthly vs annual — полные данные Recurly закрыты формой, доступен только один показатель (65% Gen Z охотнее подписываются при наличие free trial против 82% у Boomers). Стоит скачать полный отчёт.
- Доля целевой аудитории 13–24, у которой физически есть карта/платёжный метод — это определяет, нужны ли gift-card/prepaid-каналы и какой процент трафика вообще способен оплатить. Данных нет, а параметр напрямую делит воронку.
- Правила Discord по продаже внешнего ПО и по возрасту участников (13+) — support.discord.com и discord.com/creators отдали 403/404. Нужно проверить, т.к. авто-выдача роли после оплаты — несущая часть механики.
- Практический трафик-мост: сколько показов коротких видео нужно на одну оплату. Мой расчёт даёт требуемое число заказов в месяц (например, 177 подписок + 45 lifetime-продаж для $3000 net), но конверсия view→click→purchase — это измерение auto-upload, и её надо получить оттуда, а не оценивать здесь.
